# Credits

Every file under `host/art/assets/` comes from one of the packs below, and each section lists the files taken from it. The class figures, tiles, digits font and the five bosses are drawn in code (`host/art/placeholders.ts`, `host/art/bosses.ts` and `host/art/boss-data.ts`) and need no credit.

## Superpowers backgrounds asset pack

Made by Pixel-boy at Sparklin Labs for Superpowers, published at https://github.com/sparklinlabs/superpowers-asset-packs (commit e8674a0, folder `backgrounds/layers`).

Licence: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0), as stated in LICENSE.txt at the root of that repository (https://github.com/sparklinlabs/superpowers-asset-packs/blob/master/LICENSE.txt). Attribution is not required; it is given here anyway.

Files used, each colour mapped onto a colour of the game's palette (`PALETTE` in `host/art/placeholders.ts`), otherwise unchanged:

- `host/art/assets/backdrop/night-sky.png`, from `backgrounds/layers/89.png` (the night sky with clouds and the moon): the sky in night, its bands in stone deep, the clouds in stone dark, the moon dimmed to stone light with stone craters.
- `host/art/assets/backdrop/storm-hill.png`, from `backgrounds/layers/84.png` (the hill with the haunted castle and the bare trees): the hill in stone deep, a step lighter than the sky, its castle edges in stone dark and its lightning cracks in stone.
