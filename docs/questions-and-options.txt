OLKARIA: THE DEADLOCK
Fixed questions and options

Main decision maker: Microsoft (the Wizards). The case brief makes the company the primary decision-maker, so Microsoft decides last, in round 4, and the closing question is "What should Microsoft do, and why?"
Rounds 1 to 3 are the other stakeholders setting the terms. Each round is led by one class.

ROUND 1: GRID WYRM
Question: What will Kenyans accept on power?
Led by: Rogues (Citizen)
A) Households first. No data-centre load until household and industrial supply is secure; the deal waits for new generation.  [evidence 40]
B) Accept a 60 to 100 MW first phase, about 4% of peak demand, on condition that new generation is tied to every expansion.  [evidence 80]
C) Accept the full 1 GW for the jobs, the skills lab and the regional hub.  [evidence 15]
D) Only with a community deal: Olkaria communities get compensation and a share of the benefit before any expansion.  [evidence 55]

ROUND 2: DEBT GOLEM
Question: What does the Government offer instead of a guarantee?
Led by: Knights (Kenyan Government)
A) Sign the anchor-tenant guarantee. The hub is worth the risk.  [evidence 10]
B) No guarantee of any kind. Microsoft can build on market terms or not at all.  [evidence 45]
C) KenGen signs a smaller power and capacity commitment for a 60 MW phase, kept off the national balance sheet.  [evidence 65]
D) Be the customer, not the guarantor: move government services to the local region under the Cloud Policy, paid from annual budgets.  [evidence 60]

ROUND 3: FOG OF DEMAND
Question: What do Kenya's businesses commit?
Led by: Bards (Local Businesses)
A) Commit now. Safaricom, the banks and the large startups sign letters of intent to move workloads to a Nairobi region if price and latency beat South Africa.  [evidence 70]
B) Partner, don't just buy. EcoCloud and local developers co-build a 60 MW first phase and sell capacity to Kenyan firms as they migrate.  [evidence 80]
C) Wait and compare. Keep workloads with Oracle at iXAfrica, Huawei and South Africa until Azure's local pricing is known.  [evidence 40]
D) Skills first. No commitments until the innovation lab and training promised in 2024 replace the development centre that is closing.  [evidence 35]

ROUND 4: THE CORE
Question: What should Microsoft do?
Led by: Wizards (Microsoft)
A) Proceed with the full 1 GW build, taking a larger stake and securing dedicated generation.
B) Build a 24 to 60 MW facility now and expand in stages as contracts and power arrive.
C) Pause Kenya. Put the capital into South Africa and return when power, finances and demand improve.
D) Become the tenant. Local partners such as EcoCloud build and own the facility; Microsoft anchors it with Azure capacity.

Evidence scores (0 to 100) weight the damage an option deals. Round 4 has none: it scores on consistency with rounds 1 to 3.
